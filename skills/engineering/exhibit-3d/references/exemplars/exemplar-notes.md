# Exemplar notes

Four frames from the reference exhibits, captured in short browser windows, so the lede and the live caption are hidden (hud-spec §7). Open each image next to its notes. The creator's handle appears on the plinths and is never reproduced in our work.

## 01-prism-room-lab.png (Prism Room, Lab view)

- **Title column:** kicker "Where white light splits", title "Prism room" in cyan. Stats INCIDENCE 58.7°, DEVIATION 67.1°, SPREAD 15.0°; spread is the hot (cyan) value.
- **Control card:** TRY row (Split, Least bend, Trapped, Rejoin); PRISM slider with a signed value (+13.3°) next to a LIGHT slider (42°); SECOND PRISM toggle (Waiting / In); VIEW (Lab / Exploded / Studio); tool buttons for cinematic cameras and help.
- **Hero:** a white beam from a lamp on a tilt mount enters a 60° flint prism on a rotation stage and leaves as a saturated spectrum fan, the brightest thing in the frame. A small glint flare where the beam enters the glass. The fan lands on a screen at the right of the bench.
- **Console:** dark lacquer front with two big knobs (left with an amber value arc, right with a cyan ring) and two inset screens: deviation against incidence with a marker and a coral "trapped" region, and the spectrum as overlapping colored peaks.
- **Set:** a showroom at dusk. Lavender window light, a low-poly potted plant in a cream faceted pot, wall shelves of faceted crystals lit from below by warm strips, vertical amber light bars, a tiled floor, all melting into depth of field. A ghost diagram of the prism and fan floats faintly on the back wall.
- **Labels:** "N-SF11 prism · bends 67.1°" with a leader line, "Light · 42° up", "Screen · 42 nm of colour", "2nd prism · waiting". They sit inside the 3D scene and are softened by depth of field (we keep ours crisp).

## 02-prism-room-exploded.png (Prism Room, Exploded, second prism in)

- **Look preset change:** navy gradient void with a lighter haze low on the left, no room, no console. The same HUD.
- **Plinth:** wide chamfered black trapezoid, warm LED strips under the top lip and along the bottom edge, a small brass maker plate on the front. The bench top is an optical breadboard with a hole grid.
- **Apparatus pulled apart:** lamp, first prism on its stage, second prism (pulled apart along the beam) and a tall screen, spaced along the beam path. With the pair separated, colored fringes return on the screen.
- **Readouts respond to the second prism:** deviation reads 0.0° (the inverted second prism undoes the first), spread 80.2° (cyan).
- **Labels:** "2nd prism · pulled apart", "Screen · 107 nm of colour".

## 03-root-finder-lab.png (Root Finder, Lab)

- **Title column:** kicker "Root Finder", title "How roots know down" on two lines. Stats TIME 72 h (cyan), ROOT TIP 82° off ↓ in root cream, SHOOT TIP 82° off ↑ in leaf green: readout colors match the objects.
- **Control card:** TURN THE CHAMBER (↺ 90°, Upright, 90° ↻), GRAVITY (1 g, 0 g, Clinostat), TIME with a cyan play button, a scrubber with an amber event marker and ticks, speed 1× / 4×, VIEW, tools (sow again, cinematic, help).
- **Apparatus:** a clear soil chamber in a clinostat frame with big brass-and-bronze wheels, brass knobs on the corners, amber LED bars on the posts. The bean seed and soil pebbles are low-poly; the root system is smooth tubes in cream, the shoot in green.
- **Diegetic extras:** dashed vertical plumb lines with chevrons mark true down; a hint pill "Drag to turn the chamber"; a loupe titled "ROOT CAP ×40" showing cap cells with starch grains settled to the low side and a g arrow; a holographic panel on the right, "The same seedling, hour by hour", with frames at 0, 12, 24, 48 and 72 h; labels "Shoot tip · 82° off ↑", "Root tip · 82° off ↓", "Turned 8° · at 48 h", "Gravity · 1 g".

## 04-plane-of-focus-exploded.png (The Plane of Focus, lens exploded)

- **Title column:** kicker "The plane", title "of focus". Stats FOCUS 37 cm (cyan), APERTURE f/2, SHARP ZONE 0.7 cm.
- **Control card:** FOCUS (Foreground, Middle, Background), DISTANCE slider with ticks, APERTURE (f/2, f/5.6, f/16), LENS (Assembled, Exploded), cinematic button shown active, help.
- **Apparatus:** a camera lens opened along an optical rail with ruler ticks: brass gear rings, a knurled rubber focus ring, glass elements, the iris, and the image plane on a frame at the left showing the scene upside down. Thin glowing ray bundles cross the lens and converge.
- **Phenomenon:** a translucent glowing plane of focus with a bright edge, slicing through a low-poly valley diorama (pines, a cabin, mountains); everything off the plane blurs.
- **Console:** a filmstrip of five rendered photos at five focus distances with the current one ringed in cyan, a physical scrubber with ticks and chevrons, and three iris icons for the three apertures.
- **Set:** dark room with framed glowing schematic posters (a tree imaged through a lens) and floating ghost lens rings: the background echoes the topic.
- **Labels:** "Focus ring · drag ↕", "Image plane · upside down", "Plane of focus · 37 cm", "Sharp zone · 0.7 cm".
